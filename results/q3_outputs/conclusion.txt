(a) Bottlenecks d = [16, 128, 1024] with 1,515,283, 2,432,899, 9,773,827 parameters; validation MSE 0.01585, 0.00432, 0.00215. Selected AE-1024. It has the lowest validation MSE (0.00215, PSNR 26.67 dB); the next best (AE-128) is 0.00432, a gap of 95.0 pooled seed-sd, so the ranking is not seed noise. A wider bottleneck discards less information, and reconstruction error is the only criterion, so nothing here penalises the extra capacity. Validation MSE change per 8x step in d: d 16->128: 72.7% lower MSE; d 128->1024: 50.2% lower MSE. Test MSE of the selected AE-1024: 0.00217 (PSNR 26.64 dB).

(b) Gaussian noise sigma = 0.2; the DAE (same architecture as AE-1024, noisy input -> clean target, noise redrawn every batch, no labels) is compared using the fixed Q1(a) CNN. Test accuracy: clean 87.6%, corrupted 17.2%, blur 30.2%, clean-AE reconstruction 44.2%, DAE reconstruction 41.7% (ceiling: clean AE on clean images 59.9%). The DAE recovers F = 0.35 of the accuracy lost to noise (95% CI of the gain +23.3 pp to +25.7 pp), and 0.57 of what is recoverable through this reconstruction architecture. Against the clean AE it gains -2.5 pp, and against blur +11.4 pp. Decision: denoising PARTIALLY RECOVERS the information the classifier needs. Of the remaining 46.0 pp shortfall from clean accuracy, 27.7 pp also occurs when the clean AE reconstructs clean images, so most of it is the classifier's penalty for smooth reconstructions, not noise left in the image.

(c) Linear-probe test accuracy: AE-16 34.9% (MSE 0.01608), AE-128 39.9% (MSE 0.00436), AE-1024 50.7% (MSE 0.00217), DAE-1024 52.6% (MSE 0.00422); raw pixels 41.1% at MSE 0; untrained encoders d=16: 25.4%, d=128: 38.6%, d=1024: 48.6%. Spearman between reconstruction quality and class information: 0.80 (linear), 1.00 (k-NN). Decision: they PARTIALLY AGREE. Reference points: perfect reconstruction (raw pixels) gives 41.1%, less than the best trained encoder (DAE-1024 52.6%), so perfect reconstruction does not maximise linearly accessible class information. Against untrained encoders of the same width, reconstruction training changes linear-probe accuracy by d=16: +9.6 pp, d=128: +1.3 pp, d=1024: +2.1 pp. On noisy inputs the DAE representation gives 52.1% against 47.7% for the clean AE (linear probe).
