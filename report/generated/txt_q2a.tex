Under the fairness criterion fixed in advance (equal parameters: MLP-M 1,128,815 vs CNN 1,130,378; same data, no augmentation, same optimiser, schedule, epochs and batch size; same LR grid chosen on validation; 3 paired seeds), the CNN reaches 87.8\% test accuracy against 55.1\% for the MLP (+32.7 pp). The MLP fits 99.9\% of its training subset (CNN 100.0\%), so the gap is one of generalisation, not of fitting. Its first-layer units are whole-image templates while the CNN's are 3x3 detectors reused everywhere. Proposed reason: locality and weight sharing let the CNN exploit the correlations between neighbouring pixels with reusable local features, which the MLP has to relearn at every position.
