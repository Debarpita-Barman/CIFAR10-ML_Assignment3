Capacity to memorise: by epoch 150, MLP-XL predicts the assigned wrong label for 99.7\% of the 18,000 corrupted examples (shadow-label null 11.0\%), and fits 99.8\% of the clean ones, so the setting does let the MLP fit examples that cannot generalise.

Early phase: at the validation peak (epoch 6) validation accuracy is 47.7\%, clean-train accuracy is 58.5\%, and the network predicts the TRUE class for 44.4\% of the mislabelled training images (chance 10\%), even though it is trained on their wrong labels. The memorisation excess is only +5.1 pp at this point.

Memorisation onset (excess significant from then on, z > 3): epoch 0.75. By then validation accuracy had covered 59\% of its rise from chance to its peak, and true-label accuracy on the corrupted examples was 32.3\%.

Late phase: from the peak to epoch 150, the memorisation excess rises to +88.7 pp, while validation accuracy changes by -11.3 pp to 36.4\% and true-label accuracy on corrupted examples goes from a maximum of 45.3\% (epoch 3.75) to 0.1\%. After the peak, the Spearman correlation between the memorisation excess and validation accuracy is -0.12. The clean-label control with the identical recipe peaks at 57.7\% and ends at 56.6\%, so the extra decline in the noisy run comes from fitting the corrupted labels.

Per example: the median clean example is stably fitted from epoch 24, the median corrupted example from epoch 33. The same images with their true labels (control run) are fitted from epoch 14, so the delay is caused by the label being inconsistent with the image, not by the images being harder.

Test set: the early-stopped checkpoint (epoch 6, chosen on validation) scores 46.5\%, against 38.5\% for the final memorising model.

Decision: YES, useful structure is learned before memorisation begins. Generalising, class-level structure (rising validation accuracy, and the true class predicted for mislabelled images) appears while the fit to the corrupted labels is still at the shadow-label null. Memorisation of the corrupted labels starts later and then erodes that structure, as validation accuracy and true-label accuracy on the corrupted examples fall together while the memorisation excess grows.
